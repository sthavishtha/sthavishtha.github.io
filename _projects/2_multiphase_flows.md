Multiphase flows involve interfaces that move, deform, merge, and break up, often across widely separated length and time scales.

I study these phenomena using phase-field and diffuse-interface models: droplet mobility and spreading on solid surfaces, evaporation-driven dynamics, capillary pinch-off, and multiphase transport through porous and fractured media. The goal is to connect interfacial physics at the small scale to the macroscopic behavior that matters in applications.
