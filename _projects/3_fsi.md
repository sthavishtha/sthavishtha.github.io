When droplets rest on soft solids, capillary forces are strong enough to deform the substrate, and the deformation in turn reshapes the flow. This two-way elasto-capillary coupling is the focus of my current research.

I develop high-fidelity computational models that couple nonlinear elasticity with multiple immiscible fluids at deformable interfaces. One outcome of this work is *fibrotaxis*: a mechanism for gradient-free, spontaneous, and controllable droplet transport on soft solids, with applications to microfluidics, lab-on-chip devices, and self-cleaning surfaces.
